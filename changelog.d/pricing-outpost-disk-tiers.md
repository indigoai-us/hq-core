The shipped pricing copy (`pricing.json`) now lists company Outpost disk tiers under `addOns.outpost.diskTiers`: 50 GB adds $15 a month to the machine price, 100 GB adds $30, 200 GB adds $60 and 500 GB adds $150. The disk is charged only when company file sync runs on the Outpost, once the Outpost confirms it is running; without sync the Outpost gets a 30 GB disk at no charge. The pricing page states the same rule.
