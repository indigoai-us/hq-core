The scope authorizer's stdin-path check now trusts a literal echo or printf source only when its words, joined without spaces, name no company, so a reader that rejoins split words cannot rebuild a company path. A read, printf -v, mapfile or getopts whose target is PATH, ENV, BASH_ENV, SHELLOPTS or BASHOPTS now counts as changing what a command runs. `command` no longer disproves the check, since it always runs the real command.
