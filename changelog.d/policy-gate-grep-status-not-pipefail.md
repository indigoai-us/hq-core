Fixed (policy enforcement gate): a `delivery-forbid-regex` could be missed and a `delivery-require-regex` reported missing. The gate ran `printf '%s' "$BODY" | grep -Eq "$regex"` under `set -o pipefail`; `grep -q` exits at its first match, and when printf had not finished writing it died of SIGPIPE, so the pipeline returned 141 and the gate read that as "no match". A forbidden pattern near the start of a body larger than the 64 KB pipe buffer was allowed every time, and on Linux a short multi-line body lost the same race now and then. The outbound-command test, the attached-brief test and the read-receipt viewer test had the same shape. All seven now go through `grep_text`, which feeds grep the same bytes and returns grep's own status (`PIPESTATUS[1]`). Regression tests in `core/scripts/tests/policy-enforcement-gate.test.sh`: 96 KB bodies for forbid and require, a 96 KB `hq dm` command, 150 repeats of the two short bodies that failed in CI, and bodies with no final newline, one final newline, an inner blank line and no content.
