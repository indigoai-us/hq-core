Added (policy `hq-lane-outpost-choice`): before starting a lane that could run on an Outpost, the agent asks the user once where it runs, listing their personal Outposts and the lane's own company's Outposts plus "Auto (use whatever has room)" and "This machine", and maps the answer to `--outpost <id>`, `--placement auto`, or `--placement local`. A placement named in the request is used without asking, the answer is kept for the session in the `lane_placement` session value, an Outpost is never picked silently, a refused Auto is reported with the other choices offered, and unattended runs reuse an earlier placement or stay local and never pick a company Outpost on their own. `/conduct`, `/conduct-runners`, `/run-project`, the shared lane dispatch protocol, `company-outposts.md`, and `hq-suggest-outpost-uses` now point to the policy.
