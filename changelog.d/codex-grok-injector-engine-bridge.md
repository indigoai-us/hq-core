Fixed (hooks): the Codex and Grok adapters now route the policy injector (inject-policy-on-trigger) through the hq-hook engine when the hook-engine-port-inject-policy-on-trigger flag is on, with the same kill switch, engine lookup, flag pre-gate, bridge plan (minimum version and schema, which carries the engine's pin check) and `hq-hook bridge` call that master-hook.sh makes for Claude Code, plus `--host codex` or `--host grok` so the engine applies its adapter-host guard (an engine without `--host` exits 70 and the bash injector runs). Before, the flag had no effect on Codex and Grok because the adapters always ran the bash injector. With the flag off, no engine, the kill switch set, the hook left out of the plan, or an engine exit other than 0 or 2, the bash injector runs as before and nothing the engine printed is kept. The decision lives in core/scripts/lib/hook-adapter-core.sh (hq_hook_engine_*), which master-hook.sh already sources. Tests: core/scripts/tests/hook-adapter-engine-bridge.test.sh (both adapters; flag off byte-identical to the previous adapter library; on, shadow, pin mismatch, engine faults, kill switches, pre-gate agreement with master-hook.sh).
