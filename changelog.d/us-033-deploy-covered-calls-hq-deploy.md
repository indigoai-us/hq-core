Changed (deploy): `/deploy` now sends the rest of its written-out hq-deploy calls through `hq deploy` (hq-cli 5.345.238) instead of `deploy-api-request.sh` (US-033, first part of c1). These are the route-mode stale-snapshot check (`history`), the app re-reads (`app-info`), the app upload (`upload-app`, with `--acknowledge-database-billing` and `--acknowledge-public-secret-risk`), runtime bindings (`runtime-bindings list|replace`, company only), the SSR registration (`register-ssr`), the comments toggle (`comments toggle`) and database enablement (`enable-database`). The `deploy_request` helper is gone from the skill. The record step still resolves the strictest gate: it reads the app with `app-info` and, for a company app, the saved policy with `hq deploy access get`, and skips the record if either read fails. `deploy-api-request.sh` stays only for callers the CLI does not cover yet: the paged app list (`list-apps.sh`), `site-events.sh`, personal-scope `comments-watch.sh`, `deploy-record-backfill.py` and the owner comment routes. Tests: `deploy-skill-hq-cli-covered-calls.test.sh` (new), `deploy-skill-record.test.sh`, `deploy-skill-app-route.test.sh`.
