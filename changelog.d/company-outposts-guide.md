Added (outposts): a guide for company Outposts, `core/knowledge/public/hq-core/company-outposts.md`. It covers the quote and create flow (`hq outposts provision --company <slug>`, then `--yes`), `list --company` and `--outpost` when a company has more than one, vault sync and where company files live on the box, Claude sign-in (`status` link, then `login-code`), `exec` and `terminal`, scheduled jobs (`hq outposts jobs register|edit|pause|resume|delete|run-now|runs`), the member request and approval flow, `access list|grant|revoke`, `destroy`, and the limits that apply today: no lanes on company Outposts yet, no HQ DMs or email from a company job, `pull` for personal Outposts only, and changes on the box reach other devices on the next sync. New soft policy `hq-suggest-outpost-uses`: when a user creates or starts using an Outpost, the session reads at most 5 recent thread and handoff items for the active company, proposes 1 to 3 jobs tied to that work, checks which apps are usable, and asks about connecting only the apps a chosen job needs, one question at a time, through `/hq-integrations`. With nothing useful in recent work it offers a generic menu that marks each item ready or names the missing step. The `hq outposts` rows in `hq-cli-reference.md` now follow hq-cli 5.345.241 and describe company Outposts as the default; `/schedule` checks the company Outpost list for company jobs and registers them with `hq outposts jobs register`; the charter's Load On Demand list points to the new guide.
