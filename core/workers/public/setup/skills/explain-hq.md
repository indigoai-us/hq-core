# Explaining HQ at the start

This is the one explanation of HQ that setup gives. It is sent word for word
and never reworded, shortened or added to.

- **When you send it** (they asked for the explanation in their own words, or
  they clicked "Explain HQ first" and your turn does not start with a note
  from the HQ app saying it already went out): send the text under "The
  explanation" as written. Then, in the same message, one line saying you are
  starting, and the first unfinished step's one question.
- **When the HQ app sends it** (they clicked "Explain HQ first"): the app
  sends the text under "The explanation" at once, followed by the text under
  "After the explanation, when the app sends it". Your next turn starts with
  a note saying so. Do not send the explanation again.

## The explanation

**One shared brain for your company's AI.** Your clients, decisions, meeting notes, connected apps, keys and skills live in one place, and every AI that works for you reads from it and adds to it.

**It works with the agents you already use.** Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, opened in your HQ folder, already know your business. You can put agents in Slack so your team can ask them things where they already talk, and connect other agents from the HQ console under Agents. Messaging bots in this app is one more way in, not the main one.

**It's built for your team.** Invite someone and their AI knows the business on day one. Connected apps and keys can be shared with them without passing passwords around, and each company stays separate.
